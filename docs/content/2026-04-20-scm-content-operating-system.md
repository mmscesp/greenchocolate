# SCM Content Operating System

- Date: 2026-04-20
- Mode: Internal strategy with public-safe asset planning
- Objective: Launch a realistic, trust-first month-one content engine that drives qualified traffic, Safety Kit intent, and early sign-ups without sounding generic or facilitative
- Audience:
  - Primary: respectful Barcelona visitors who want to understand the system properly
  - Secondary: expats and locals looking for a more credible, verification-first resource
  - Tertiary: journalists, researchers, and policy-curious readers who amplify credible work
- Active skill stack: `scm`, `scm-growth-marketing`, `scm-editorial-seo`, `scm-brand-voice`, `scm-legal-safety-guardrails`, `spreadsheet`

## Executive Call

The draft calendar has energy, but it is too generic, too busy, and too close to competitor behavior.

Main issues:

1. It assumes a small media team, not a solo founder.
2. It overweights generic Barcelona travel bait instead of SCM-specific authority.
3. It includes risky wording such as "This is NOT illegal," "how to join one," and "find and join."
4. It chases follower growth harder than qualified trust, site visits, Safety Kit intent, or sign-ups.
5. It asks for too many net-new assets, which will push quality down fast.

Main decision:

Month one should run on a lean system:

- 3 net-new content assets per week
- 1 email per week
- 1 website article every 2 weeks
- 1 Reddit post every 2 weeks
- stories as light consistency, not full production projects
- TikTok only as selective repurposing, not a second full content machine

This gives SCM enough output to look alive, useful, and serious without burning you out.

## What To Keep From The Draft

- Strong instinct to use carousels for saves and authority
- Good instinct that stories should stay lightweight
- Correct belief that trust beats hard-selling
- Useful separation between attract, educate, engage, and convert

## What To Cut Or Rewrite

- Daily-ish posting cadence
- Generic "Barcelona hits different" travel filler
- Early club spotlight dependence before enough verified material exists
- KPI obsession with followers over qualified actions
- Trend-chasing as a core system
- Hashtag-heavy planning as a growth strategy

## Month-One Strategic Frame

SCM should behave like a premium editorial navigation brand, not a weed page and not a tourist hype account.

The content job in month one is:

1. Correct dangerous misunderstandings
2. Build trust through specificity
3. Show that SCM has a method, not just a map
4. Move the right readers toward the Safety Kit and sign-up

Never judge the month by total posts. Judge it by net-new production units.

Weekly cap:

- 1 reel

- 1 email

## Production System

Each week starts from one central source asset.

Example flow:

1. Write one hero angle
2. Turn it into the carousel
3. Expand or adapt it into the article or email
4. Pull one reel hook from the same source
5. Use story Q&A to mine next week's objections and questions

This means you are not "making 6 things."
You are making 1 strong idea and distributing it well.

## Asset Blueprints

### Hero Carousel

- Goal: saves, shares, profile trust
- Slide pattern:
  - slide 1: sharp misconception or tension
  - slide 2: legal or civic context
  - slide 3: what people usually get wrong
  - slide 4: practical interpretation
  - slide 5: what to do instead
  - slide 6: Safety Kit or SCM method CTA
- Primary CTA: `Get the Safety Kit`

### Reel

- Goal: reach plus profile visits
- Structure:
  - first 2 seconds: contrast statement
  - middle: 2 to 3 compact truths
  - close: one line that points to SCM
- Best reel themes:
  - Barcelona is not Amsterdam
  - private does not mean risk-free
  - selected beats unvetted

### Secondary Authority Post

- Goal: sharpen brand edge and trust
- Formats:
  - founder note
  - myth vs reality
  - red flags checklist
  - what SCM checks

### Email

- Goal: repeat attention and site return
- Structure:
  - one sharp opener
  - one lesson or correction
  - one useful link
  - one CTA only

## Voice Rules For Public Assets

Use:

- direct, compact, specific wording
- legal context before access language
- contrast framing
- calm, premium, protective tone

Avoid:

- stoner slang
- "best clubs" hype
- "instant access" language
- "join now" or "find and join" phrasing
- generic tourism-blog filler

## Legal-Safety Notes For This Calendar

High-risk draft phrases that should not ship:

- `This is NOT illegal`
- `Here's exactly how to join one`
- `Find them all on SCM`
- `Tap the link to find it`
- `We found one you'll want to know about`

Safer rewrites:

- `The legal reality is more nuanced than most visitors think`
- `What respectful visitors should understand before they rely on any club information`
- `Use SCM to start with a more careful filter`
- `See how SCM approaches verification`

Disclaimer to use on articles, emails, and high-risk captions:

`SCM provides information, not legal advice. The legal landscape for cannabis social clubs in Spain is complex and evolving. Always verify club status independently and consult local legal resources if in doubt.`

## External Context To Anchor The Content

Use these as internal planning anchors, not as hype:

- EL PAIS reported on 2024-07-10 that Barcelona had ordered the first 30 closures in its campaign against cannabis clubs.
- ICEERS said on 2024-07-12 that inspections had already led to sanctions and closure orders affecting clubs in Barcelona.
- Barcelona City Council said on 2025-04-25 that it carried out 45 actions in 30 establishments in 2024 tied to cannabis-derived products, reinforcing the wider enforcement posture.
- Competitor-style pages still use facilitative language such as "request an invitation" or "join one today," which gives SCM a clear opportunity to win on credibility instead of imitation.

- update KPI sheet

## Final Operating Rules

1. Do not build the content engine around generic Barcelona aesthetics.
2. Do not publish anything that a careless reader could screenshot as access facilitation.
3. Do not chase volume when the stronger move is sharper differentiation.
4. Do not launch club spotlights until the verification standard and relationship quality are both real.
5. Always build from one weekly source idea into multiple outputs.
