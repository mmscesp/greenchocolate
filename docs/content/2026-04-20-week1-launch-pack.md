## Legal-Safety Review

- Risk classification: MEDIUM
- Why: access-adjacent category, public-facing launch content, tourism-facing audience
- Required disclaimer: include on carousel caption, reel caption, and email footer when possible
- Blocked language:
  - `join now`
  - `find the best club`
  - `guaranteed access`
  - `secret spots`
  - `instant entry`
- Safe positioning line:
  - `SCM is a better filter for people who want to understand Barcelona properly.`
